### Project Learnings

**What I enjoyed**

I enjoyed automating the research process. Starting with around 300 companies and identifying only relevant accounts using Clay was fascinating.

**Biggest Challenge**

The biggest challenge was completing the project using trial credits. I had to be careful with every enrichment, which made the process longer.

**What I Learned**

I learned that GTM research is not about finding more companies, but identifying, qualifying, and prioritizing the right ones.
