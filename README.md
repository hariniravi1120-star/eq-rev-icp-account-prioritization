# EQ-REV ICP Account Sourcing & Prioritization

## About EQ-REV

EQ-REV helps brands grow on quick-commerce platforms like Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket. It uses data and marketing insights to help brands improve their performance and presence on these platforms.

## What I Built

I wanted to understand how a GTM team can go from:

“Here is a long list of companies”

to:

“These are the companies we should contact first.”

So, I built a simple account sourcing and prioritization workflow for EQ-REV.

I started by sourcing Indian B2C companies with 50–500 employees. I then researched whether they were already present on quick-commerce platforms such as Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket.

I added more relevant signals, scored the companies based on their relevance and potential, and finally grouped them into priority tiers.
