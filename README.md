### EQ-REV ICP Account Sourcing & Prioritization

**About EQ-REV**

EQ-REV helps brands grow on quick-commerce platforms like Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket. It uses data and marketing insights to help brands improve their performance and presence on these platforms.

**What I Built**

I wanted to understand how a GTM team can go from:

“Here is a long list of companies”

to:

“These are the companies we should contact first.”

So, I built a simple account sourcing and prioritization workflow for EQ-REV.

I started by sourcing Indian B2C companies with 50–500 employees. I then researched whether they were already present on quick-commerce platforms such as Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket.

I added more relevant signals, scored the companies based on their relevance and potential, and finally grouped them into priority tiers.

**Workflow**

```text
Find companies
      ↓
Research and qualify them
      ↓
Add useful signals
      ↓
Score each account
      ↓
Prioritize who to contact first
```

**The goal:**
The goal was not to build the biggest possible list of companies. The goal was to build a repeatable system that could help answer: Which companies look like the best potential accounts for EQ-REV, and why?

**How I approached it:**
I used **Apollo** to source an initial list of Indian B2C companies and **Clay** to research and enrich them.
For each company, I looked at questions such as:

Is the brand already selling on quick-commerce platforms?

How visible is the brand across these platforms?

Are there any publicly observable growth or commercial signals?

Does the company appear to have the business capacity to invest, based on revenue and funding data?

I then converted these signals into a weighted score out of 100 and used the score to group accounts into Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3.
