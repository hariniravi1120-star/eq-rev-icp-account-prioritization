### Scoring Methodology

Each company was scored out of 100 based on five signals.

| Signal | Maximum Score |
|---|---:|
| Q-Commerce Qualification | 40 |
| Q-Commerce Visibility | 20 |
| Growth Intent | 10 |
| Revenue | 20 |
| Funding | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Why these signals?

**Q-Commerce Qualification — 40 points**

This was given the highest weight because EQ-REV works with brands on quick-commerce platforms. Companies already present on these platforms are therefore more relevant potential accounts.

**Q-Commerce Visibility — 20 points**

This measured publicly observable evidence of a brand's presence and visibility across major quick-commerce platforms.

**Growth Intent — 10 points**

Public signals such as hiring, expansion, marketing activity, or quick-commerce-related activity were used as indicators of potential commercial momentum.

**Revenue — 20 points**

Revenue was used as a proxy for a company's potential business capacity.

**Funding — 10 points**

Recent or significant funding was used as an additional signal of financial capacity and growth potential.

**Final Score**

The five scores were added together:

Q-Commerce Qualification Score + Q-Commerce Visibility Score + Growth Intent Score + Revenue Score + Funding Score = EQ-REV ICP Score

**Account Tiers**

- **Tier 1:** 80–100
- **Tier 2:** 60–79
- **Tier 3:** Below 60

The scoring model is intended to help prioritize accounts for outreach. It is not a prediction of whether an account will become a customer.
